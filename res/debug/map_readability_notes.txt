Map readability — before/after notes (Prompt: map + void fairness)

BEFORE
- Wall/prop art sat at full brightness on busy victorian cobble → blockers hard to parse.
- Interior Wall colliders relied on light prop tiles alone (no silhouette base).
- Fallen cells were flat black rectangles → same value as cobble grout (looked like floor paint).
- Dark floor-chip decals (gid 264–268) could fake solid walls.
- Nest isolation was only soft corridor weighting; voids could cut a nest off early.

AFTER (verified)
Obstacles
- Props tint ~0.55 and Walls Layer ~0.32 (multiplicative) so masonry/props sit darker than cobble.
- Charcoal-brown footprint under every Rectangle Collider (matches F1 white boxes).
- auditColliderPropCoverage: 8/8 interior Wall rects have Props gid at center — 0 mismatches.
- Boundary walls remain on the rim under Walls Layer (not invisible interior walls).
- Decals A/C soot-only (no dark floor chips). Regenerated via --patch-nests.

Abyss
- Fallen cells: warm masonry lip + recessed shelf + pitch core + N/S depth shading.
- Overlay re-stamp after falling quads so holes stay readable mid-drop.
- Cracking telegraph: amber outline + crack lines (still obvious).
- Player center on fallen → EXTRACTED (abyss) + short rumble + “EXTRACTED” float.
- Enemies on fallen cells destroyed with reward=false (no +time).

Fairness
- Fountain stamp protected; nest pads ±2 while ratio >= 0.3, else ±1.
- Fountain→nest and nest→nest corridors while ratio >= 0.22.
- Crack picks reject cells that would isolate an uncleansed nest from the fountain.
